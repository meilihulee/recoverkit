Where do I download each report?
  • Inventory Ledger: Reports → Fulfillment → Inventory → Inventory Ledger → Detailed view, last 18 months.
  • Inbound Shipments: Shipping queue → each shipment → Contents → Download (or the Shipment Items export).
  • FBA Customer Returns: Reports → Fulfillment → Customer Concessions → FBA customer returns.
  • Refunds: Reports → Payments → Date Range Reports → Transaction, last 18 months. We read only rows of type Refund.
  • Fee Preview: Reports → Fulfillment → Payments → Fee Preview.
  • Optional — My Measurements: a CSV with sku, true-longest-side, true-median-side, true-shortest-side, true-weight (inches, pounds). Without it we check Amazon's dims against Amazon's own fee.

Questions sellers ask

Do you see my data?

No. The audit runs in JavaScript inside your browser tab. Nothing is uploaded; you can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and it still works.

How accurate are the dollar figures?

They're estimates. Inbound and warehouse units are valued at a share of your list price (you set it). Amazon pays on its own valuation, so recovered amounts vary. Fee checks use an editable, approximate rate table; confirm against the current rate card before filing.

Doesn't Amazon reimburse automatically now?

For some cases, yes. Many still need a case opened with evidence, and windows are short. The claim CSV includes the evidence line and suggested case text for each one.

What if a finding was already reimbursed?

Check it against your Reimbursements report before filing. Reimbursement matching is on our roadmap.

RecoverKit is not affiliated with Amazon. Estimates only; verify before filing.
